Shanghai is gearing up for a massive weekend of culinary exploration. From high-end Michelin-recommended treats on the Bund to immersive African cultural festivals and fusion coffee experiences in Lujiazui, the city is hosting eight distinct food markets designed to celebrate the flavors of the season.

**BFC The Bund: 'Starry Road' Food Market**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_2.webp)

Foodies can indulge in a "Michelin on the Street" experience at BFC. This massive gathering features over 100 brands, including 10 Bib Gourmand-recognized restaurants from the Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Shanghai regions. Expect a wide range of regional and international flavors, alongside avant-garde creations like "West Lake Vinegar Fish Coffee" and artisanal desserts.

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_3.webp)

*   **Time:** May 23–24, 10:00–22:00
*   **Location:** BFC The Bund, 5F Sky Garden & Fengjing Road (600 Zhongshan East 2nd Road, Huangpu)

**Rainbow Century: African Beat Night**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_4.webp)

Transport yourself to the African continent at Rainbow Century. This immersive event features high-energy African music, traditional dance performances, and authentic African cuisine, offering a cultural escape without leaving the city.

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_5.webp)

*   **Time:** May 22–24
*   **Location:** Rainbow Century Moon Bay (188 Rainbow Road, Hongkou)

**Lujiazui International Coffee Culture Festival: Spring Special**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_6.webp)

Under the theme "An Oriental Seat," this festival merges specialty coffee with traditional Chinese aesthetics. A major highlight is the lakeside performance in Shanghai's first urban park, featuring the "Prince of Kunqu," Zhang Jun. The show, "New Kunqu Melodies," blends 600 years of tradition with electronic rhythms, set against the backdrop of Lujiazui's skyscrapers.

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_7.webp)

Visitors can enjoy camping-style tea sessions, intangible cultural heritage workshops, and a trendy design market, all while sipping premium coffee on the lawn.

*   **Time:** Now through May 31
*   **Location:** Lujiazui Central Greenery (717 Lujiazui Ring Road, Pudong)

**PAC Leisure Coffee & Dessert Market**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_8.webp)

For those with a sweet tooth, PAC is hosting over 30 coffee and dessert brands. Expect everything from pour-over coffee and creative lattes to gourmet sandwiches and delicate pastries in a relaxed, musical atmosphere.

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_9.webp)

*   **Time:** Now through May 24, 11:00–20:00
*   **Location:** PAC Shopping Center (688 Changde Road, Jing'an)

**HAI550: FIZZY BIZZY**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_10.webp)

Wine lovers can explore hundreds of varieties, including reds, whites, and sparkling wines. The event offers professional tastings paired with gourmet food stalls and live music for the perfect afternoon of "tipsy" relaxation.

*   **Time:** May 22–24
*   **Location:** HAI550 (550 Huaihai Middle Road, Huangpu)

**Xinhua Lane Weekend Market**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_11.webp)

Hidden among historic villas, this market offers a cozy, less crowded alternative. It features global street food, handmade delicacies, and fine desserts, making it an ideal spot for families and couples to soak up Old Shanghai's leisurely charm.

*   **Time:** May 22–24, 12:00–19:00
*   **Location:** Lane 345, Xinhua Road, Changning

**Hongqiao Food Street: "Early Summer Evening Breeze" Trunk Market**

Experience a trendy trunk market featuring light snacks, beverages, and handmade crafts. It's the perfect way to unwind and hunt for unique trinkets in the cool evening air.

*   **Time:** May 22–24
*   **Location:** Hongqiao Food Street (1899 Huqingping Highway, Qingpu)

**"Vibrant Changning" Food Week**

Calling all burger enthusiasts! This dedicated food week brings together top-tier burger brands. From classic American beef burgers to experimental limited editions, it's a one-stop destination for burger lovers.

*   **Time:** Now through May 24
*   **Location:** Hongqiao Art Center (888 Tianshan Road, Changning)
