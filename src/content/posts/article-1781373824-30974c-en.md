Explore the 'Long江' fantasy circus in Chongqing, now officially promoted by authorities. The show emphasizes safety with sterilized venues and strict health protocols, including mask mandates and no smoking. Attendees must follow one-ticket-per-person rules, with children under 3 excluded unless paying adult prices.

Refunds are non-negotiable except in cases of show cancellations or ticket errors. Entry and exit times are regulated, with ticket exchanges allowed until 30 minutes before performances. Mobile phone use is restricted, and live streaming is prohibited.

For inquiries, contact 023-62873666 or 023-62630360 during business hours. 

Operators stress no animals will perform in this show, despite unrelated social media content.
