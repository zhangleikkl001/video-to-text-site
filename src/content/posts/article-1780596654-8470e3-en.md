On May 19-21, 2026, the IMEX Frankfurt exhibition took place, highlighting Beijing's growing influence in global conferences. Beijing's cultural and tourism department led a delegation of 10 top enterprises, leveraging air transport resources and new visa policies to promote Beijing as a premier international convention and tourism hub.

**Strategic Focus on International Collaboration**
This initiative underscores Beijing's efforts to build a global exchange center, combining cultural heritage with modern exhibition facilities. The delegation emphasized high-end tourism products and seamless entry policies, with China Airlines showcasing direct routes from Beijing to Frankfurt.

![](/images/article/article-1780596654-8470e3/image_1.webp)

**Cultural Immersion and Policy Benefits**
Interactive zones featuring intangible cultural heritage and Peking opera outfits allowed visitors to experience Beijing's traditions. The exhibition also detailed updated visa policies, including 50 countries enjoying visa-free entry and 29 with mutual visa exemptions.

**Expanding Global Partnerships**
Through specialized briefings and partnerships with 300+ international firms, Beijing secured 3,800 potential visitors. The focus on Europe and South America aimed to create tailored tourism packages under new policy advantages.

**Future Outlook**
With ongoing policy improvements and the upcoming CIMX conference, Beijing plans to enhance its global brand as a convention and tourism leader, strengthening its role in international cultural and economic exchanges.
