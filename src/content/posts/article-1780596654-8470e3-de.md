On May 19-21, the 2026 International Conference and Award Tourism Exhibition (IMEX Frankfurt) took place in Germany. Beijing's cultural and tourism agency led a delegation of 10 top local enterprises, leveraging flight resources and new entry policies to showcase the city's strengths as an international convention and tourism hub. 

![](/images/article/article-1780596654-8470e3/image_1.webp)

The event emphasized strategic development of international exchange centers, combining Beijing's cultural heritage with exhibition resources. 

![](/images/article/article-1780596654-8470e3/image_2.webp)

Immersive cultural experiences, including traditional costumes and craft workshops, highlighted Beijing's unique charm. 

![](/images/article/article-1780596654-8470e3/image_3.webp)

Through targeted outreach, 300+ international partners signed cooperation intents, with 3,800+ potential visitors. 

![](/images/article/article-1780596654-8470e3/image_4.webp)

Beijing aims to strengthen global partnerships and solidify its position as a top-tier convention and tourism destination.
