This weekend, Shanghai's streets buzz with food markets, from luxury dining at the BFC Business Center to African cultural experiences. Eight distinct markets offer diverse culinary adventures across the city.

**BFC 外滩金融中心**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_2.webp)

Dubbed the 'Street Michelin,' this market features 100+ food brands, including 10 Shanghai-based Michelin-recommended restaurants. Highlights include creative 'West Lake Vinegar Fish Coffee' and 'Kill Cake' desserts.

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_3.webp)

Dates: May 23-24, 10:00-22:00
Location: BFC Tower's 5th-floor Sky Garden & Mao'an Road (Yellowpu District, No. 600 Zhongshan East 2nd Road)

**瑞虹新天地非洲节拍夜**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_4.webp)

Immerse in African culture with live music, traditional dances, and authentic food stalls. Experience Africa's unique charm without leaving China.

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_5.webp)

Dates: May 22-24
Location: Rhonghong New World Moonlit Bay (Rhonghong District, No. 188 Rhonghong Street)

**陆家嘴国际咖啡文化节**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_6.webp)

The 'One Seat Eastern' theme blends coffee with traditional culture. Highlights include aquatic performances of Zhang Jun's modern Kunqu opera and spring tea experiences with camping setups.

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_7.webp)

Dates: Ongoing until May 31
Location: Shanghai World Trade Center Green Space (Pudong, No. 717 Lujiazui Park Loop)

**PAC 自在咖啡甜品市集**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_8.webp)

30+ coffee and dessert brands offer pour-over coffee, specialty drinks, and pastries. Enjoy music and interactive activities in a relaxed atmosphere.

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_9.webp)

Dates: Ongoing until May 24, 11:00-20:00
Location: PAC Shopping Center (Jiang'an District, No. 688 Chengde Road)

**HAI550 FIZZY BIZZY**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_10.webp)

Sample 100+ wines from red, white, and sparkling varieties. Professional tastings and food pairings available.

Dates: May 22-24
Location: HAI550 (Huaihai Middle Road 550, Huangpu District)

**新华巷周末烟火市集**

![](/images/article/article-1781287516-74d11d/image_11.webp)

Cozy indoor market in a historic building with global street food, handmade treats, and desserts. Perfect for families and couples.

Dates: May 22-24, 12:00-19:00
Location: New Hua Street 345, Changning District

**虹桥食尚天地“初夏晚风”后备箱市集**

Focus on light bites, handmade crafts, and evening refreshments. Bargain for unique items while enjoying summer breezes.

Dates: May 22-24
Location: Hushang Tian Di (Qingpu District, No. 1899 Huxing Plain Road)

**“烟火长宁 幸福集惠”活力美食周**

Hamburger lovers' paradise with diverse local brands. From classic American thickers to limited-edition creations.

Dates: Ongoing until May 24
Location: Vanguard Art Center (Langning District, No. 888 Tianshan Road)
